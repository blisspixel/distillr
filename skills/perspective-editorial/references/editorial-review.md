# Review procedure

Read the brief, captured evidence, and rewritten article together. A revision
passes only when every criterion below passes. Use model judgment for meaning;
use exact checks for dates, numbers, names, links, and copied evidence.

| Criterion | What the reviewer must establish |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Material factual claims are supported, qualified, dated, and attributed. Company announcements are not independent test results. |
| Perspective | The interpretation fits the stated person or company and audience without inventing experiences, policies, or endorsements. |
| Reader value | The article answers a useful question and supports a specific takeaway beyond a summary of announcements. |
| Structure | The opening establishes value; sections develop the argument without repetition; the ending earns its conclusion. |
| Voice | Word choice, specificity, tone, and level of detail reflect the authorized profile and examples. |
| Prose | Sentences are natural and precise, with no filler, inflated certainty, repetitive patterns, emojis, or em dashes outside indispensable quoted evidence. |
| Completeness | The requested scope and format are covered, important caveats are present, and no required stage is falsely claimed complete. |

For each criterion, record `pass`, `revise`, or `abstain`, the reason, and a short
exact article excerpt showing the issue or the basis for passing. For important
factual claims, also record the source URL and a short exact supporting source
excerpt. Clearly separate inference from reported fact. A link alone is not
support. Do not invent numeric scores as a substitute for findings.

When a quotation was copied incorrectly, check the actual source and repair the
review record once. This repairs clerical evidence errors only. A missing or
contradictory source calls for an article revision, a qualified claim, or removal.
Never alter a quotation to make it support the draft. Keep excerpts brief and
respect source quotation limits.

Revise the article against the findings, then recheck the changed claims and the
seven criteria. Preserve the original and revised text. Stop after the configured
bound, normally two rounds; return the draft and unresolved findings if needed.
Abstention means the reviewer lacks adequate evidence, not permission to guess.

Use a separate reviewer model when available and authorized. If the same model
performs review, describe it as a separate review pass. Model agreement is not
proof of correctness. Do not claim watermark removal or AI-detector guarantees.

Before export, compare links and factual values against the receipts, inspect
title and heading hierarchy, remove process notes and unrequested attribution,
and confirm that final files actually exist. If DOCX rendering is available,
inspect all pages for clipped text, broken links, awkward breaks, duplicate
titles, and empty pages. State any export or visual-review limitation plainly.
